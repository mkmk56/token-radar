Valuation Model & Category Calibration

Pre-token protocols are valued by anchoring their TVL to empirical market multiples derived from tokenized peers in the same category, adjusted for growth velocity, maturity age, and multi-chain breadth.

Core Formula

implied_value_usd = max(1, round(tvl_usd × category_multiple × growth_mult × maturity_mult × chain_mult))
  • Category Multiple: Median Mcap/TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category, winsorized to [0.02, 2.00]. Falls back to global median if fewer than 3 live peers exist.
  • Growth Multiplier: 1 + (clamp(change_7d, -60, 120) / 100) × 0.5. Missing growth counts as 0%.
  • Maturity Multiplier: 0.6 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4 (unknown listed_at defaults to 365 days).
  • Chain Breadth Multiplier: 1 + 0.05 × min(chains.length - 1, 4), capped at 1.20.
